# ToneCustoms Community Code of Conduct

ToneCustoms aims to maintain professional and constructive spaces for FiveM development, documentation, support, and community feedback.

## Expectations

- Treat members, contributors, and customers with respect.
- Discuss ideas and problems constructively; avoid harassment, threats, hateful language, and personal attacks.
- Keep issues and discussions relevant, accurate, and free from spam or misleading promotions.
- Respect intellectual property. Do not redistribute paid or private source files without authorization.
- Protect privacy: do not post customer information, passwords, access tokens, license keys, private code, or server credentials.
- Report security vulnerabilities privately using [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than publishing exploit details.
- Follow GitHub's applicable platform rules.

## Moderation

ToneCustoms maintainers may close off-topic issues, moderate discussions, and restrict participation where GitHub permits when necessary to maintain a safe, productive environment. Feedback and feature suggestions are welcome but do not guarantee implementation.

## Reporting concerns

Use GitHub's reporting tools for inappropriate content or contact [ToneCustoms Discord](https://discord.gg/JdZ7NTG5KF) to request a private communication channel. Do not share sensitive details publicly.

For routine customer assistance, see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
