## ABOUT TONECUSTOMS

**ToneCustoms is an independent FiveM development brand dedicated to building resources that bring more depth, interaction, and purpose to roleplay servers.**

We believe a great roleplay experience is shaped by the details: the way a player interacts with the world, how a job feels from beginning to end, how server systems support the community, and how reliably those systems fit into everyday gameplay.

Our work goes beyond adding features to a server. We focus on creating **practical, configurable systems with meaningful player interactions**—from civilian activities and character progression to weapon mechanics, server management, and specialized roleplay experiences.

ToneCustoms is built on hands-on experience developing and managing a FiveM roleplay server. That perspective influences how we approach each resource: with attention to the player experience, the needs of server owners, and the real work involved in operating a community.

**Our goal is straightforward: create FiveM resources that serve a purpose, contribute to better roleplay, and are presented with clear information and dependable support channels.**

## OUR APPROACH

### Designed Around Roleplay

We prioritize interactions that give players something to do, decisions to make, and experiences that feel connected to the world around them. A resource should add to the roleplay—not simply add another menu.

### Built for Server Owners

Every server has its own direction, economy, and gameplay expectations. We favor configurable systems and documented dependencies so owners can understand what a resource does and how it fits their environment.

### Quality Over Rushed Releases

Development, testing, documentation, and release approval are separate steps. We do not treat every commit, prototype, or showcase as a finished product. Public releases and customer-facing updates are published when approved—not on an artificial schedule.

### Clear Documentation & Support

Product information should be easy to find and easy to understand. We maintain dedicated documentation, installation guidance, troubleshooting information, and official support channels so customers know where to go for help.

## HOW WE WORK

A resource should make sense from the first interaction to the final result. Our development approach starts with the roleplay experience and carries through implementation, review, documentation, and approved release.

| Stage | What matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Concept & Purpose** | Define the player experience or server problem the resource addresses |
| **System Design** | Establish the interaction flow, dependencies, configuration, and boundaries |
| **Development** | Implement the system with attention to usability, consistency, and server integration |
| **Review & Refinement** | Evaluate the complete gameplay loop, handle issues, and refine the experience |
| **Documentation** | Explain installation, relevant requirements, supported behavior, and limitations |
| **Approved Release** | Publish customer-facing builds and updates only after owner approval |

## OUR COMMITMENT TO CUSTOMERS

**Professional development includes more than the resource itself.** We aim to give customers clear expectations about the product they are considering and a straightforward way to get help after purchase.

**Product Transparency** — Documentation explains intended functionality, relevant dependencies, and known limitations when established. Features still in development are identified as such.

**Installation & Configuration** — Available guides explain how to prepare, install, and configure the relevant resource. Compatibility information is product-specific rather than a blanket promise.

**Customer Support** — Assistance with installation, configuration, and product questions is available through our official support community. We do not promise a fixed response or resolution time.

**Updates & Maintenance** — Approved updates are communicated through official channels. We do not guarantee a particular update schedule or imply that every development change becomes a customer release.

**Licensing & Distribution** — Our [customer policy center](https://github.com/ToneCustoms/tonecustoms-docs/blob/main/policies/README.md) explains applicable licensing and distribution expectations. Current checkout terms and applicable law govern purchases.

## DEVELOPMENT & ORGANIZATION

This GitHub organization is the home for ToneCustoms development repositories, public documentation, issue tracking, and business standards. Projects are organized independently so their code, documentation, and progress remain manageable.

**Our development principles:**
- Protect private source code, customer information, credentials, and proprietary assets.
- Keep documentation and public product descriptions aligned with approved functionality.
- Track changes without treating every commit as an official release.
- Review and approve releases before customer distribution.
- Maintain a consistent identity across GitHub, documentation, support, and the storefront.
